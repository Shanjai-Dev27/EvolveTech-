Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Which students can join Evolve Academy?

Our programs are designed for school students from 7th to 12th standard and for college students.

Where are the sessions held?

We run workshops and awareness programs directly at schools and colleges. Centre-based and online sessions are also available, depending on the program.

How can a school or college book a workshop?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or email, or fill in the enquiry form below. Tell us the class level, the topic and the number of students, and we will plan the session with you.

What do students take away from a program?

Our programs are hands-on. Students learn by building and testing real projects, so they leave with practical skills and something they have made.

What kind of solutions does Evolve Studio build?

We build AI-powered applications, websites and web applications, automation-based applications, IoT monitoring systems, robotics and embedded system solutions, and custom software.

How do I start a project with Evolve Studio?

Contact us with a short description of your idea. We discuss your needs, plan the work, build the solution and support it after launch.

What do the programs cost?

Fees and schedules depend on the program, the group size and where the sessions are held. Contact us and we will share the details.
